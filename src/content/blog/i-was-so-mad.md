**Monday**

I have been here before.  It was a really easy day for me.  The classes are significantly smaller than others.  Some students were here for multiple periods if I remember correctly.  There is an aide for the whole day, and some students have one on one aides.  Today the lesson plan is very simple.  They are expected to do a free write for a warm up.  After that, there is a program called EdPuzzle they will do on President's Day.  So far as I know this program shows a video like YouTube would, but it pauses at certain points to allow the students to answer questions.  I believe sometimes there can be questions at the end of the video, too.  I usually hear students do not like the program.  

Two class periods are called Strict Literacy.  This makes me think it is a made up class for supposed extra help with understanding.  Public education is failing to educate on what will improve society.  Teachers are not allowed to teach, students are not able to learn, people are creating weaker people.  It is a system that is so well thought out that the founders were able to recreate themselves and build a system that creates more of them.  It is recruiting and building a loose empire without the new ones even realizing it.

One of the students, perhaps is demon possessed.  I won't get into details, but it was like watching the movie, Split.

**Tuesday**

The class was very bare.  One of the students said that they had moved classrooms a couple of weeks before.  There wasn't even a whiteboard.  There was one of those all in one Newline screens, a large, commercial printer, and teacher desk.  The student desks were just tables and chairs.  It was certainly depressing, and I do hope that if I go there again, there will be decorations and more equipment more suitable for a high school class.  I completely understand that the teachers were transitioning to a new room and may not have had time or ability to move everything.

Later I found out their real classroom flooded one day and this class they are in now is temporary.

**Wednesday**

This class was interesting.  The teacher was an athletic director or some type of coach.  She did not have classes for the last 3 periods of the day, at least nothing I needed to do as a substitute teacher.  I almost got a full day's pay for just four class periods.  I ended up sticking around for a little while to finish up a report.  In that time, I was asked to stay for the 7th period.  I was bummed out, a little, since I saw an opportunity to leave early.  It also is a sign of some inefficiency when I potentially could have got paid for a full day, when my services as a substitute were not necessarily needed for a full day.  I have heard this school district does not use funds well.  I did end up getting to leave 30-40 minutes early, which is still a great feeling.  I love to be able to beat the traffic.  

It's bittersweet.  I know that getting paid a few hours for not working is not cost effective.  I have to remind myself why I am getting paid.  When I was a resident substitute teacher, I spent a few hours of the day, with no tasks or responsibilities during the 2020/2021 school year.  I was getting paid to be available. The biggest problem is that I see that this cannot be sustainable for too long.  Any company or organization that is not using their funds well, will have consequences.  It is possible that I would get a pay cut, or even reduced opportunities to pick up assignments.  I am keeping that option open.  This job and pay may not last as long as I think it will.

I do not want to get to the point of feeling entitled to get paid for a full day when I do not work the entire set of hours.  Is that socialism?

**Thursday**

I've been to this class a few times, and I didn't remember it being too bad.  The room has a seating chart, the kids are mostly reasonable, until the last two class periods.  I often have a decent time because this class has two other adults to help out.  Today, one of those co-teachers was not there.  I also found out that most of the security guards and administration team was out as well.  I had problems at this school before.  I did not feel supported by the staff.  This class has a last period prep.  I was expecting to be able to leave early again.  I was not able to.  The parking and traffic are not too bad, it is still just that great feeling of being able to leave without restrictions. 

During 5th and 6th period, I had a real struggle.  One student simply had her phone out and around the campus I see banners that say, "This is a no phone school."  I gave her a warning, and I even understood why she was on her phone.  I told her to use the passing period to talk with her parents.  She did.  I suppose it wasn't enough time to get everything sorted out.  She used her phone again, and then refused to leave it in the teacher's desk drawer until the end of the period.  I had to write her a referral.  

Another student simply would not stop talking in class, across the room and negatively about himself and other students.  He was out of his seat for most of the period.  Another boy was similar.  I wrote them up as well.  I understand that the students are just trying to survive.  They do not know how to do the work they are doing.  They do not see the value in it.  They just want to have some fun and grow their interpersonal skills by socializing at mandatory school.  Unfortunately, they are learning some terrible behaviors and it excites them.  They are growing more and more wild, as is human nature.

One more student was just not doing his work.  He gave me a little attitude when I asked him to get to work.  I was very irritated at that point and I may have lashed out at him a bit by writing him up.  I will say that I did want to make a point, too.  I did not want to simply allow the students to do no work and have no consequences.  I am sure most of those students will not know why they got in trouble.  They won't understand, and simply blame me as a crazy older guy who lost it in class.  I have heard them talk about other teachers and students.  I cannot imagine that they have only positive things to say about me.

I had to make 3 phone calls to the same person to get someone to escort these students out.  Supposedly there was a fight during lunch, which I understand is a higher priority when there is limited staff.

After all this, I now believe I may have encountered a demonic spirit.  One student, as far as I know, took the work, the packet, and just put it away.  He did absolutely no work in class.  The next period he was supposed to do work on the computer.  I don't know if he even logged on to the computer.  He was talking and laughing with others.  While I was getting the other students out, he started to ask me questions to hear my reaction.  He was sitting all the way on the other side of the room, by the door.  He was staring at me, watching.  I made a mistake of telling him to come sit closer to me, for some reason.  I didn't want to send another student out or write another student a referral.  

He kept asking me questions and making comments.  Mostly, I ignored him.  Other students asked for help with their work, one or two asked to go to the restroom.  I could not help them or let them leave at that time.  There is a wisdom in being in a class and when one is in authority over others, you get a bit of a sense of why a person asks to leave.  Most of the time, there is a real reason to leave, to the restroom or get water.  No issues.  Sometimes, and at least once a day, I am sure, a person will ask to leave because they need to get out or want to avoid something.  It is a needed break or also a type of avoidance.  It felt that way for those requests.  They may have felt uncomfortable, or they just wanted to get out to do something they liked better.  It is possible, some of them wanted to get out so that they could use their phones to report to someone else what was happening in their class to gossip or for their own documentation.

This boy was watching to see what I did next.  Looking for an opportunity to make me more upset.  I felt it.  It was not a good feeling at all.  I felt helpless in being able to do anything.  I wanted to put the fear of God in these students, but I know that would not have been the best choice at the time.  He was taunting me, he still did no work at all.  At one point he was standing nearby and it seemed like he could see what I was typing on the referrals.  Mostly for privacy, and to assert more authority, I stood up and walked directly towards him and told him to walk away.  He backed down as soon as I stood up, and another student made a comment.  I could only think that if this were a movie, or a much older way of living, like the wild west. This boy would be not have survived long.  The type of behavior he showed is not something I would put up with.  I thought of the word "punk," to describe that boy.

I did not have enough support to get the student to leave, I could not physically move him myself, I did not want to show weakness in that I just pushed my problems away.  It was hard.  The students know and feel what they can get away with in their behavior.  Teachers and school staff are powerless to do any real good.

This class period is another example of why the school system is failing.  I think there is some responsibility on the teacher's part, but it is ultimately a failing system to begin with.  I already talked about the system as a whole, created to create more like itself.

About half-way through the 5th or 6th period, I get a folder with an assignment for the 7th period.  This class was for the co-teacher who was missing!  I was expected to go back to a room with the same students I just left.  Some of them, at least.  I made a few attempts to get out of this last period assignment.  I was going to tell the secretary that I was not going to do that last period.  Reduce my pay, do whatever, but I was not going to go into that class.  I tried to give her some time to find a replacement, but when I got to the office, she was gone.  The only person there was busy with a parent.  I looked toward the class and I saw the other adult who was with me for 1st period.  I asked if she was going to be there.  She was.  There was a high probability I was going to walk out of that class if I was the only one in it, especially because some of the students I sent out were back in that class.  She stayed and seemed to experience the same type of behavior I was seeing, a wildness.  She did her best to control and direct them, but I could tell she was with limited power, and she was distressed.  She appeared firm, but I know that she was there every day.  That was her job.  

She experienced this behavior daily.  A nightmare.  It is traumatic.  I wouldn't have seen it as such, if I didn't leave my job as a teacher about 5 years ago, November 2021.  I was in trauma, and didn't know it.  I feel so bad for her that she is there in such an environment.  The teachers in any school are the ones who determine how the class runs.  It can be very difficult to do anything contrary to how the rest of the school may run. It is not impossible.  I have seen classes run very well, without the teacher.  As a substitute teacher, I can only do so much with a group of kids I may not see again for months or ever.  When I walk in and the kids are respectful, getting their work done, and either pleasant or just quiet, I know that the teacher has control, love, mercy, and understanding. Or, the teacher is an absolute dictator that controls with extremely strict rules.  That is much more rare, and the kids usually make some sort of comment about how glad they are to have a substitute teacher.
