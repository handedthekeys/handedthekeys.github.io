Week 6 2026

Monday

This class was different.  It was a math class, for the most part.  There was a co-teacher for four of the periods and one class period I was on my own.  The classroom looked very much taken care of.  I could see many different decorations and even hand written posters of math concepts to serve as guides for the students.  I think I even saw some class rules as well.  I know I saw the school bell schedules written out and big enough for all to see.  I didn't have to do much since the co-teacher was there.  She took over the class and I helped just a little.  During two class periods I passed out little tickets for students who were participating in the whole group class instruction and for the students who elected to put their phones away for its duration.  



I did have to get called away for the prep period for a teacher who had a meeting.  I was all set to watch the class for him, but he came back in around 10 minutes, letting me know the meeting was cancelled.



I saw a four students from my elementary teaching days.  One was from my exact class that I taught all year.  I even used to see her at my local grocery store sometimes.  This school year, when I first saw her, I sensed she was trying to avoid me.  I made a bit of a joke about it to her.  Only time will tell later what's going on.  I really like to see my past students again.  

I also got to speak to a student I met last year at a different school.  The last time we spoke we talked about origami, her experience at a new school, and how her environment affected her focus and grades.



Tuesday

This English class is very relaxed.  I have a decent lesson plan.  The students are respectful and they seem to miss their teacher.  I had no issues whatsoever.  This teacher has a messy desk.  Not in a filthy way, but there are piles of papers all around, organized, but piles.  I can tell this teacher is either very disorganized, or it might be a sign of high intelligence.  

The room does not have much for decorations, but there is an interesting decoration that looks like a 2'x15' collection of student collages.  I see a few college pennants, too.  

This school has announcements every Tuesday morning.  One thing they do is say the entire Pledge of Allegiance over the intercom.  I have noticed that almost no classes I have been in do all the students stand.  Even fewer of them do I hear all the students reciting as well. 

There was almost no trash on the floor by the end of the day and I only had to put back one of the classroom computers.  This tells me the students definitely respect or fear this teacher.

Prep period was the last one of the day, so usually that means I get to leave early, beating any traffic.

Wednesday

This class runs like clockwork!  The students come in, quietly, grab their journals and start writing for the first 10 minutes.  There is a seating chart and they stick to it.  Once they are done writing, they come up to me so that I can initial that they wrote a whole page.  The teacher also leaves the day's work written on the board so the students simply have to look up to know what to do.  There are clear classroom rules on the board as well.  The students follow the rule.  In this environment, it feels like the teacher is very strict. The desks and computers are all numbered.  Each student takes the computer assigned to them by desk number.  Excellent!

There are some decorations around the room, not too much, but it seems to fit the environment.  She has a floor heater too, so nice!

One class period was not an honors class right before lunch. One student came in late and immediately started cursing because someone was in her seat.  She called him a name, the rest of the class was relatively quiet.  She didn't come in with a backpack.  It seemed like she wanted attention.  She had a couple friends show up late, too.  They sat together, talked and avoided their work.  Almost all the rest of the class was working on their journals, while that group talked loud enough for all to hear.  One boy removed himself from the area.  I almost made him go back since he did not ask permission, but then I realized from where he was removing himself.  I commended him for taking action.  He still didn't do much of his work.

That same girl kept cursing, I had to write her a referral to the office.  I did this because I wanted a record of her behavior.  I know that many teachers deal with so much disrespect and poor behavior and it just goes unchecked.  I usually just maintain the rules of the classroom.  I understand that as a visitor to any class, especially if I am most likely only going to be there for one day, I cannot expect the students to fully adjust to my expectations, but I can hold them to the standards that the teacher has already set in place.  I am looking for which rules the teacher actually enforces and which are ignored.  This rule was posted on the classroom whiteboard, along with no eating or drinking.  I assumed that meant no drinks other than water. I warned this girl a few times.  It wasn't a lot of work for me to write a referral, but I still did not want to do it.  I did find a way to do it that was a bit easier.  I was able to email it to the teacher and an administrator.  That way, there is a digital paper trail.  I wonder sometimes when I have written paper referrals, that maybe they were just thrown away.



Thursday

This class is well decorated.  It is a math class for 9th and 11th graders.  During 1st period, one of the girls was drawing on the desk.  I noticed and made sure to talk to her.  I just asked if she was going to erase it later.  She said she just needed hand sanitizer and it would come off.  By the middle of the class I saw her trying to get rid of the drawings.  It looks like she used water and a napkin.  She was scrubbing away to get it off and in an almost panic.  I could not find any hand sanitizer for her.  

Lesson plan:  Very simple. Each class has a half sheet of math problems to do. This assignment should take only 5-10 minutes if the students understand the concepts.  The 9th grade classes are small, which is nice. The desk chair I am using only has the right arm rest.  I guess that's okay, but it confuses me a little.  

Past students:  I have three of them in this class today.  There is also Mordacai.  I spoke to this boy at another school earlier this year.  If I remember correctly, he remembered me from the previous year.  He was at a new school and he missed his friend who was in a grade below him.  He wanted me to help him find her because he broke his phone and didn't have her number.  We talked about how he could make some money to fix his phone.  It was a PE class and the assignment was to walk laps around the track for the class period, in summer. We talked for a good amount of time.  He told me her name and told me the school she was going to.  I made no promises, but I did write down her name as he spelled it.  It wasn't until early January, that I finally ended up at the school in the right class.  He had her last name spelled wrong, I almost didn't notice her.  I asked her if she had a friend by that name and she seemed to light up a little.  I told her he was looking for her, but didn't have a phone.  I could tell she was impressed.  

Today, I saw Mordacai again, and was able to let him know that I spoke to her.  He was a little excited, but it turns out that he still doesn't have enough to fix his phone.  In passing, one day, I thought I saw him a week ago or so, with a phone, but it could have been a different student, or a friend's phone.

In all this, I don't want to be a meddler or really a middle man, that's why I didn't make any promises or really exchange any information for them.  I suppose I could have given them their phone numbers or something, but I don't want to be that involved.  It is a nice story to see play out.  I am hesitant because it seems pretty easy to me to be able to get a new free phone somehow, get on a social media platform of some kind, and find each other.  It is the task of the boy to seek out the girl, perhaps he is just not thinking clearly enough to find a solution.  There may be other reasons they are not together that he is not telling me as well.  Right now he is struggling to get the phone.  I suppose we all have a struggle that seems easy to others.

Another student I used to have, sorta.  His mother died during the school year.  It was awful.  I heard him say he did not like that school year.  Almost anyone would understand that.

One student seems to always be late to class no matter where I see her. A few students asked to go to other classes.  Another girl asked for the work to take to her friend.  I suppose she is going to make her do her work, being a good friend.  

One girl came in late, wearing a JROTC uniform.  She looked very well dressed and proper.  She was late, with a pass from the office.  A little while later, she started talking, cursing, and complaining.  It ruined her whole appearance.  Sad.  She must have been distressed.  She then just walked out of class.  I followed and there she was talking with two boys.  The boys said they were just walking by, then they said they were waiting for someone in the next room.  She said no one in the class was bothering her and if she was in the class she would just get loud.  It makes no sense.  She did not want to go into a corner by herself or just come back in to put her head down.

As simple as the work was today, I see that at least one student is copying the answers of another.  Can you believe it?  It is so much easier now for students to copy.  When a teacher assigns paper work, students can take photos of it, use AI, and get answers.  Then, they send a photo to their friends to copy.  It is crazy!  I think this is a failure on the part of education, although public education is already failing if you look at what it says it was supposed to do.  If you look at what it is actually for, it is a major success!  Look deeper, why do we require kids to leave their families to go be with strangers for most of their days, to learn the hidden curriculum that they will not need?  I won't go too much deeper now, but if you look into it, there are, I think, two main reasons for free public education.  One is good, the other is evil.

This school uses colored clip boards for restroom passes. It seemed like a great idea, instead of using small passes students could easily hide.  The issue I have seen is that these clip boards are plastic and some have already broken.  Now, obviously, the students are not taking care of them but perhaps next year the school will consider more sturdy objects.  I have seen some awesome restroom passes in the past.  My favorite is probably the 3'x3' sheet of wood for a third grade class. Another noteworthy one is a toilet seat on a string.

Random thought:  Restrooms are pretty important.  I remember watching a scene in the show Seinfeld.  Geroge Costanza was bragging about his knowledge of the best restroom facilities in the city.  It was so strange to me, but now as an adult. I get it.  No one wants to walk into a restroom that has a terrible odor, broken amenities, or a lack of supplies.  So, for the most part, any school I go to I start to figure out which restrooms I would use.

I like to use the single-use restrooms.  The perfect one would have warm water, paper towels, and the door should open outwards.
